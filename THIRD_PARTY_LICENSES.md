## 5. Summary of Cleaned / Removed Assets

The following unneeded or unverified third-party dependencies have been audited and removed:

- ❌ **Third-Party CDN CDNs / Remote Asset URLs**: None present. All assets are self-contained and bundled within the local Next.js project.
- ❌ **Proprietary Fonts / Unverified Audio / 3D Asset Files**: None present. All typography is Open Font License (OFL-1.1).
